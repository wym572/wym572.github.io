TweeTok 是一款独立开发的第三方媒体浏览工具，帮你以全屏沉浸或双列瀑布流的方式浏览你在 X 上关注的图片和视频内容，提供 iOS、tvOS 和 Android 版本。

常见问题

提示需要重新登录，或时间线突然加载不出来

这通常是 X 的会话凭据过期了。凭据由 X 签发并控制有效期，我们无法延长。请在「我的 → 账号管理」中退出该账号后重新登录。如果反复出现，可能是该账号在 X 侧触发了风控，建议先用浏览器登录 x.com 确认账号状态正常。

时间线是空的

请确认该账号在 X 上确实关注了一些用户。若你正在查看的是「列表」时间线，请确认该列表内有成员且成员近期有发帖。也可以下拉刷新重试。

视频不播放，或只有画面没有声音

请先确认设备没有开启静音开关。若视频始终停在加载状态，多为网络问题，切换网络后重试。个别推文的视频可能已被作者删除或设为受限，此时在 X 官方应用中同样无法播放。

Apple TV 配对失败（仅 iOS）

请确认三点：iPhone 与 Apple TV 连接在同一个 Wi-Fi 网络；已在 iOS 的「设置 → TweeTok」中允许「本地网络」权限；已允许「相机」权限以扫描二维码。若仍失败，将两端应用都完全退出后重开再试。

如何清除缓存和本地数据

  • 图片缓存：「我的 → 设置 → 清除图片缓存」
  • 已读记录：「我的 → 设置 → 已读筛选 → 清除已读记录」
  • 离线缓存的推文：在离线缓存列表中左滑单条删除，或点右上角「清除全部缓存」
  • 登录凭据：退出登录即从设备上清除
  • iOS 版的离线文件也可以在「文件」App 的「我的 iPhone → TweeTok」中查看和删除

关于我在分析服务上的数据

使用分析以设备上随机生成的 ID 上报，其中包含你浏览和操作的帖子 ID 与作者 ID，详见隐私政策。退出登录会清除该账号的随机 ID，再次登录会换成新的 ID。由于应用不显示这个 ID，我们目前无法按你的身份找到并删除特定记录。

为什么需要相机和本地网络权限（仅 iOS）

相机仅用于扫描 Apple TV 上显示的配对二维码；本地网络用于向已配对的 Apple TV 传输账号会话，以及连接局域网内的 WebDAV 服务器。拒绝授权不影响 iPhone 上的正常浏览。详见隐私政策。

TweeTok Pro 和恢复购买

TweeTok Pro 是一次性购买，后续 Pro 功能更新都包含在内。付款由 Google Play 或 App Store 处理。换设备或重装后，请用购买时的账号登录应用商店，在 TweeTok Pro 页面点「恢复购买」。两个平台的购买相互独立，不能互通。退款请通过购买所在的平台申请。

云端同步怎么用

云端同步是 Pro 功能，使用你自己的 NAS 或支持 WebDAV 的存储。在「我的 → 设置 → 云端同步」填写服务器地址、用户名和密码即可。数据只发送到你填写的服务器，我们无法访问。如果地址是未加密的 http，同一网络中的其他人可能看到这些内容，建议使用 https。

如何举报内容或拉黑账号

点击帖子的「更多」（⋯），选择「举报或拉黑」。被举报的帖子和被拉黑账号的帖子会立即从你的信息流中移除，我们也会收到通知。儿童安全问题请同时参阅儿童安全标准。

如何删除账号

TweeTok 不创建账号。在「我的 → 账号管理」中可以把账号从应用移除。要删除 X 账号，请在 X 上操作。

能发推文吗

本应用定位是浏览工具，支持点赞、收藏、转发、引用转发和评论，但不支持发布原创推文。

TweeTok is an independently developed third-party media viewer that lets you browse the photos and videos from the accounts you follow on X, full screen or in a two-column grid. It is available for iOS, tvOS and Android.

Frequently asked questions

I'm asked to sign in again, or the timeline suddenly stops loading

Your X session credentials have most likely expired. They are issued and expired by X, and we cannot extend them. Sign out of the account under "Me → Account Management" and sign in again. If this recurs, the account may have been flagged on X's side — sign in to x.com in a browser first to confirm the account is in good standing.

My timeline is empty

Check that the account actually follows someone on X. If you are viewing a List timeline, check that the list has members and that they have posted recently. Pull to refresh to retry.

A video won't play, or plays without sound

First check that your device's silent switch is off. A video stuck loading is usually a network issue — switch networks and retry. Some videos may have been deleted or restricted by their author, in which case they will not play in X's own app either.

Apple TV pairing fails (iOS only)

Check three things: your iPhone and Apple TV are on the same Wi-Fi network; Local Network permission is granted under iOS Settings → TweeTok; and Camera permission is granted so the QR code can be scanned. If it still fails, fully quit both apps and try again.

How do I clear caches and local data?

  • Image cache: Me → Settings → Clear Image Cache
  • Read history: Me → Settings → Read Filter → Clear Read History
  • Cached posts: swipe to delete individually in the Offline Cache list, or tap Clear All Cache
  • Credentials: signing out clears them from your device
  • On iOS, offline files can also be viewed and deleted in the Files app under "On My iPhone → TweeTok"

About my analytics data

Usage analytics is reported under an ID generated randomly on your device and includes the IDs of posts you view or act on and of their authors. See the Privacy Policy. Signing out clears that account's random ID, and signing in again produces a new one. Because the app does not display this ID, we currently cannot find and delete specific records based on your identity.

Why does the app need Camera and Local Network access? (iOS only)

Camera is used solely to scan the pairing QR code shown by the Apple TV app. Local Network is used to transfer your session to a paired Apple TV and to reach a WebDAV server on your local network. Declining them does not affect normal browsing on iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

TweeTok Pro and restoring purchases

TweeTok Pro is a one-time purchase, with all future Pro feature updates included. Payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store. After changing devices or reinstalling, sign in to the store with the account you purchased with and tap "Restore Purchase" on the TweeTok Pro screen. Purchases on the two platforms are separate and do not carry over. For refunds, please apply through the store where you purchased.

How do I use cloud sync?

Cloud sync is a Pro feature that uses your own NAS or WebDAV storage. Enter the server URL, username and password under Me → Settings → Cloud Sync. Data is sent only to the server you enter, and we cannot access it. If the address is unencrypted http, others on the same network may be able to see this data, so https is recommended.

How do I report content or block an account?

Tap "More" (⋯) on the post and choose "Report or Block". Reported posts and posts from blocked accounts are removed from your feed immediately, and we are notified. For child safety concerns, please also see our Child Safety Standards.

How do I delete my account?

TweeTok does not create accounts. You can remove an account from the app under Me → Account Management. To delete your X account, please do so on X.

Can I post from the app?

TweeTok is a viewer. You can like, bookmark, retweet, quote-tweet and reply, but composing original posts is not supported.
