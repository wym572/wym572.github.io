常见问题

提示需要重新登录，或时间线突然加载不出来

这通常是 X 的会话凭据过期了。凭据由 X 签发并控制有效期，我们无法延长。请在「我的 → 账号」中退出该账号后重新登录。如果反复出现，可能是该账号在 X 侧触发了风控，建议先用浏览器登录 x.com 确认账号状态正常。

时间线是空的

请确认该账号在 X 上确实关注了一些用户。若你正在查看的是「列表」时间线，请确认该列表内有成员且成员近期有发帖。也可以下拉刷新重试。

视频不播放，或只有画面没有声音

请先确认设备没有开启静音开关。若视频始终停在加载状态，多为网络问题，切换网络后重试。个别推文的视频可能已被作者删除或设为受限，此时在 X 官方应用中同样无法播放。

Apple TV 配对失败

请确认三点：iPhone 与 Apple TV 连接在同一个 Wi-Fi 网络；已在 iOS 的「设置 → TweeTok」中允许「本地网络」权限；已允许「相机」权限以扫描二维码。若仍失败，将两端应用都完全退出后重开再试。

如何清除缓存和本地数据

  • 图片缓存：「我的 → 设置 → 清除图片缓存」
  • 已读记录：「我的 → 设置 → 已读筛选 → 清除已读记录」
  • 离线缓存的推文：在离线缓存列表中左滑单条删除，或点右上角「清除全部缓存」
  • 登录凭据：退出登录即从设备的 Keychain 中清除
  • 离线缓存的文件也可以通过 iOS「文件」App，在「我的 iPhone → TweeTok」中直接查看和删除

如何删除你在分析服务上的数据

发邮件到 wym572@gmail.com，附上你的 X 用户名，我们会在 30 天内删除与该账号关联的全部分析记录。详见隐私政策。

为什么需要相机和本地网络权限

相机仅用于扫描 Apple TV 上显示的配对二维码；本地网络仅用于在同一网络内向已配对的 Apple TV 传输账号会话。两项权限都不会用于其他目的，拒绝授权不影响 iPhone 上的正常浏览。详见隐私政策。

能发推文吗

本应用定位是浏览工具，支持点赞、收藏、转发、引用转发和评论，但不支持发布原创推文。

Frequently asked questions

I'm asked to sign in again, or the timeline suddenly stops loading

Your X session credentials have most likely expired. They are issued and expired by X, and we cannot extend them. Sign out of the account under "Profile → Accounts" and sign in again. If this recurs, the account may have been flagged on X's side — sign in to x.com in a browser first to confirm the account is in good standing.

My timeline is empty

Check that the account actually follows someone on X. If you are viewing a List timeline, check that the list has members and that they have posted recently. Pull to refresh to retry.

A video won't play, or plays without sound

First check that your device's silent switch is off. A video stuck loading is usually a network issue — switch networks and retry. Some videos may have been deleted or restricted by their author, in which case they will not play in X's own app either.

Apple TV pairing fails

Check three things: your iPhone and Apple TV are on the same Wi-Fi network; Local Network permission is granted under iOS Settings → TweeTok; and Camera permission is granted so the QR code can be scanned. If it still fails, fully quit both apps and try again.

How do I clear caches and local data?

  • Image cache: Profile → Settings → Clear Image Cache
  • Read history: Profile → Settings → Read Filter → Clear Read History
  • Cached posts: swipe to delete individually in the Offline Cache list, or tap Clear All Cache
  • Credentials: signing out clears them from the device Keychain
  • Offline files can also be inspected and deleted directly in the iOS Files app under "On My iPhone → TweeTok"

How do I delete my analytics data?

Email wym572@gmail.com with your X username and we will delete all analytics records linked to that account within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why does the app need Camera and Local Network access?

Camera is used solely to scan the pairing QR code shown by the Apple TV app. Local Network is used solely to transfer your session to a paired Apple TV on the same network. Neither is used for anything else, and declining them does not affect normal browsing on iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I post from the app?

TweeTok is a viewer. You can like, bookmark, retweet, quote-tweet and reply, but composing original posts is not supported.
