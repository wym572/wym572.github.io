TweeTok（下称「本应用」）是一款独立开发的第三方媒体浏览工具。本政策说明本应用收集哪些信息、为什么收集、存放在哪里，以及你如何控制这些信息。

一句话概括：你的 X 登录凭据只保存在你自己的设备上，我们看不到，也永远不会上传。但本应用确实会把匿名之外的使用数据——包括你的 X 用户 ID、handle，以及你浏览了哪些推文——发送给第三方分析服务 PostHog。详见第 2 节。

1. 本应用不做什么

  • 不收集、不接触、不传输你的 X 账号密码。登录全程在 X 官方网页中完成。
  • 不运营任何存放你内容或凭据的服务器。
  • 不出售、不出租、不交易任何个人数据。
  • 不做跨应用或跨网站的广告追踪，不使用 App Tracking Transparency 追踪权限，不接入任何广告 SDK。
  • 不收集你的位置、通讯录、日历、健康或财务数据。

2. 本应用收集什么

2.1 X 登录凭据（仅存于你的设备）

你在应用内的 X 官方登录页完成登录后，本应用会保留 X 返回的会话凭据（auth_token、ct0、twid），用于代表你请求你自己的时间线内容。

  • 存放位置：你设备上的 iOS Keychain，由系统加密。
  • 是否上传：否。这些凭据不会发送给我们或任何第三方，唯一的去向是 X 自己的服务器。
  • 例外：若你主动使用「连接 Apple TV」功能，凭据会在你的本地网络内、在你的两台设备之间直接传输，不经过互联网。

2.2 使用分析数据（发送给 PostHog）

本应用使用 PostHog 分析功能使用情况，数据发送至其位于美国的服务器（us.i.posthog.com）。上报内容包括：

类别具体内容用途
账号标识你的 X 用户 ID 与 X 用户名（handle）区分不同用户的使用情况
浏览行为你查看的推文 ID、该推文作者的 ID 与用户名、所在时间线、是否含媒体了解内容消费方式，优化浏览体验
操作事件点赞、收藏、转发、评论、翻译、缓存、搜索、切换时间线、进入全屏等动作了解功能使用率
外链点击你在应用内点开的推文外部链接地址诊断链接打开失败
设备与应用信息设备型号、系统版本、应用版本、语言、启动与前后台切换事件兼容性与稳定性排查

请特别注意：上述分析数据与你的 X 用户 ID 相关联，因此不是匿名的。这意味着 PostHog 上存有一份「某个 X 账号浏览过哪些推文」的记录。我们不会将这份数据用于分析之外的任何目的，也不会与第三方共享，但你有权知道它的存在。如果你不接受这一点，请不要使用本应用。

2.3 保存在你设备上的本地数据

以下数据只存在于你的设备，不会离开设备：

  • 你主动离线缓存的推文正文、图片与媒体封面（存于应用的 Documents 目录）
  • 图片与视频的临时缓存
  • 已读记录、时间线偏好、筛选设置等本地偏好
  • 多账号列表

这些离线缓存文件可以通过 iOS「文件」App，在「我的 iPhone → TweeTok」中直接查看、导出或删除，无需经过我们。

TweeTok (the "app") is an independently developed third-party media viewer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can control it.

In one sentence: your X credentials stay on your own device and are never uploaded to us. However, the app does send non-anonymous usage data — including your X user ID, your handle, and which posts you view — to the third-party analytics service PostHog. See section 2.

1. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, handle or transmit your X password. Sign-in happens entirely on X's own web page.
  • It does not operate any server that stores your content or credentials.
  • It does not sell, rent or trade any personal data.
  • It does not perform cross-app or cross-site advertising tracking, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • It does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, health or financial data.

2. What the app collects

2.1 X credentials (device only)

After you sign in through X's official login page inside the app, the app retains the session credentials returned by X (auth_token, ct0, twid) in order to request your own timeline on your behalf.

  • Where: the iOS Keychain on your device, encrypted by the system.
  • Uploaded? No. These credentials are never sent to us or to any third party; the only destination is X's own servers.
  • Exception: if you choose to use "Pair with Apple TV", the credentials are transferred directly between your two devices over your local network. They do not traverse the internet.

2.2 Usage analytics (sent to PostHog)

The app uses PostHog to analyse feature usage. Data is sent to its servers in the United States (us.i.posthog.com) and includes:

CategoryDetailsPurpose
Account identifierYour X user ID and X username (handle)Distinguish usage between users
Browsing behaviourIDs of posts you view, the ID and username of each post's author, which timeline it came from, whether it contains mediaUnderstand content consumption, improve the browsing experience
Interaction eventsLikes, bookmarks, retweets, replies, translations, caching, search, timeline switches, entering fullscreen, and similar actionsUnderstand feature adoption
Outbound linksThe URL of any external link you open from a postDiagnose link-opening failures
Device and app infoDevice model, OS version, app version, language, launch and foreground/background eventsCompatibility and stability

Please note carefully: this analytics data is linked to your X user ID and is therefore not anonymous. It means a record exists on PostHog of which posts a given X account has viewed. We do not use this data for any purpose other than analytics and do not share it with third parties, but you are entitled to know it exists. If this is not acceptable to you, please do not use the app.

2.3 Local data on your device

The following never leaves your device:

  • Posts, images and media posters you explicitly cache for offline use (stored in the app's Documents directory)
  • Temporary image and video caches
  • Read history, timeline preferences and filter settings
  • Your list of signed-in accounts

These offline files are browsable directly in the iOS Files app under "On My iPhone → TweeTok", where you can inspect, export or delete them yourself without going through us.

  • Access and correction: you may request a copy of the analytics data linked to you.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK you additionally have GDPR rights to object to and restrict processing and to data portability. If you are a California resident you have CCPA rights to know and to delete. We never sell personal information.
